0002 · Merge reports only when the cause matches

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-11-08 · 0 days ago

Date: 8 November 2026 · Scope: this reviewed kb export.

Context

Ticket #4471 combined different date failures because the desk matched subject lines. Its old “one issue, one fix” description hides a booking-v3 form-hint defect behind a shared-parser defect. Intake tags are guesses, not verified causes. Reports kept arriving after the ticket was marked known.

Decision

This record replaces decision 0001. The accepted original is retained unchanged for history.

Read the report and establish its cause using tenant locale, form version, submission channel and supporting reproduction evidence. Merge only when that cause matches the target ticket. A matching subject is insufficient; different subjects do not prevent a merge. Unknown tenants or unresolved causes remain separate for investigation.

For the supplied date reports, use the executable classification rather than browser locale or intake tags. Deduplicate export rows by report ID before counting. Preserve anomalous reports and their uncertainty; classification does not prove that a suspected mechanism explains every symptom.

Consequences

Triage takes more reading than matching subjects, but avoids concealing a separate failure behind a popular ticket. Keep one engineering issue per supported cause, with unique report IDs, counts and evidence. New reports must still be triaged while engineering works; “known” is not a reason to merge. If evidence changes, reclassify and recompute counts without losing provenance.

The updated runbook applies this rule. The incident review links the resulting work.
